4. PRIVACY

The Software does not send your audio anywhere. It processes sound from your
turntable only on your computer, to play it and to detect when a side starts and
ends.

What leaves your computer, and only when the related feature is used:
  - album searches and album look-ups (to MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive);
  - what you're currently playing (to Discord, if enabled);
  - songs you've played and when (to ListenBrainz and/or Last.fm, if connected);
  - your Discogs username and record look-ups (to Discogs, if you import from it);
  - a version check (to GitHub).

Your settings, listening history, record shelf, cached album covers and any
sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer (in %APPDATA%\TurntableListener).
Sign-in tokens are encrypted using your Windows account. The author does not
collect, receive or sell any of your data. Uninstalling the Software and deleting
that folder removes it all.
